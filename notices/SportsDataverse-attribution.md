# College football data attribution

Source: SportsDataverse / cfbfastR contributors, original college football game data as identified by the publisher.

Data release store: https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data

Publisher: https://github.com/sportsdataverse/cfbfastR-cfb-data

Data license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The release-store README identifies the data license as CC BY 4.0, separately from software-package licensing.

Integrated release tags: cfb_team_info, cfb_schedules, espn_cfb_team_box, espn_cfb_pbp. This application filters FBS/FCS membership and completed games, aggregates team and split statistics, and computes its own opponent-adjusted summaries, rankings and baseline scoring models. These are modifications, not official or endorsed league statistics.

Original team names and trademarks remain with their owners. No logos or proprietary grades are distributed. Source availability and completeness are not guaranteed.
